# 5 — MVP for your daughter

[Overall roadmap](../roadmap.md) · Previous: [chapter](04-chapter.md)

**Goal:** deliver an installed build that is comfortable to play on the priority device. Depends on the complete chapter. Releasing here means making it available to your daughter; not publishing on a store.

This milestone finalizes and optimizes the production already integrated in milestones 3b–4. It does not concentrate the modeling of the whole game: follow [characters P4](3d-characters.md) and [environment A4](3d-environment.md), with the [inventory](../3d-production.md) up to date.

## Learn

Find performance problems on the real device, prepare a reproducible build and distinguish game blockers from optional improvements.

## Proposed tasks

- [ ] List what is still provisional and replace only what is necessary for the final experience.
- [ ] Finalize the beaver, scenery, buildings and essential animations, keeping the shapes readable at the camera distance.
- [ ] Finalize voice and sounds. Allow repeating instructions and adjusting the volume; use image and sound together.
- [ ] Review touch areas, contrast, icon size and clarity of targets on the chosen device.
- [ ] Check the chapter on that device: movement, interaction, transitions, pause, exit, resume and ending. If it is Android, include suspending and returning to the application; if it is the PC, loss of focus and closing the window.
- [ ] Observe performance over a complete session and fix stutters that hurt the controls. Define the technical target based on the device, without promising performance on untested hardware.
- [ ] Repeat the technical rehearsal from milestone 3b with the complete chapter and compare with the target and the measurements recorded there; performance validation does not start only in this milestone.
- [ ] Try it with your daughter and first fix blockers, loss of progress, confusing controls and insufficient cues.
- [ ] Keep the origin and license of the models, sounds and other assets used.
- [ ] Prepare an identified build, short instructions for her dad to install/open it and a recoverable backup of the project.
- [ ] Verify all the delivery criteria of the overall roadmap on the installed build.
- [ ] Record ideas for after the MVP without introducing them in this delivery.

## Completion criterion

Your daughter can play, complete the chapter with the help of the game itself, resume progress and show the valley. Her dad can open or reinstall the build without rebuilding the project in the editor. A reinstall must not be used as a test on the only copy of her progress: preserve that progress first.

If the test shows disinterest or confusion, go back to the corresponding milestone to adjust the experience. More content or more detailed art do not replace an understandable interaction.

**After the MVP:** decide with you whether the next goal is to improve the chapter, support the second device with the same care, test with other families or prepare a public release.
