**Goal:** gradually replace the provisional assets with the chosen presentation, preserving the validated play. Depends on milestone 3a, on the animation test during startup and on the decision about the origin of the art and finish by the end of milestone 2.

**Current status:** closed for sequencing at the author's request. The visual set was exercised on the POCO with simulated taps, but full milestone validation has not been completed: the 15-minute adult rehearsal, listening checks, hands-on obstacle checks, priority-device choice and child observation remain open. Unchecked tasks below remain outstanding.

## Completion criterion

Beaver and stretch of the valley work in the installed build, the owl presents the help and the otter marks the forming of the lake without blocking the interaction. A recognizable otter in a static celebration pose or animated by code satisfies this criterion; there is no need to wait for its own rig or animations to move on to home and food. The interactions and saving from 3a remain correct, the presentation is readable and there are no recurring stutters that hurt movement or touch in the recorded rehearsal.

This milestone validates the visual quality we can produce and maintain; it does not require reproducing the finish of the v3 illustration.
